# HoosHelping (beta)

HoosHelping connects members of a university community who need a hand with members who offer one, starting with
childcare and rides. This TestFlight build is a **demonstration**: every other member, request and post is
synthetic, and the build has no server. Nobody you match with is a real person, and no one will come to help.

## What to try

- Post a request for now or later.
- Set what you can help with and when.
- Compare offers with ratings and badges, confirm a match and chat with them.
- Ask a chosen helper directly from the People directory.
- Join the community forum, and report or block any member.

Please use made-up details only.
