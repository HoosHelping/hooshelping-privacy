# HoosHelping beta: privacy statement

*Applies to: the HoosHelping **demonstration** build distributed through Apple TestFlight. Last updated: [DATE].*

**This beta is a demonstration.** The members, requests, chats and forum posts you see are made up. Nobody you
"match" with is a real person, and no one will come to help.

## What this beta collects

**Nothing.** The beta has no server. What you enter (your name, a sign-in email, requests, offers, messages,
forum posts, ratings) stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Please use **made-up details only**. Do not enter real information about yourself, your children, your home or
anyone else.

## What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to send yourself:

- **Feedback email.** If you tap a feedback button, your mail app opens a message to us with the app version,
  your phone's OS version and model, and the build type. It does not include anything you entered in the app.
  You can read and edit it before sending.
- **TestFlight feedback.** Screenshots and comments you send through TestFlight go to the developer through
  Apple. Apple's own privacy policy covers that channel.

## Location, contacts and notifications

The beta never reads your location or your contacts. Any notifications it shows are generated on your phone; it
registers for no push service.

## Third parties

The beta contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software and shares nothing with third parties.

## Deleting your data

Open **Home → Delete account** to erase everything the beta stored. Deleting the app also removes it.
